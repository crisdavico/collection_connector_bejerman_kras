Kras's debt, sellers and seller commission base, read from its Bejerman
query views.

Adds the ``kras`` value to **Bejerman Profile**
(``collection_connector_bejerman``) and acts only on connectors of that
profile; connectors of other customers in the same database are
untouched. Installing the addon marks every Bejerman connector that
already exists as ``kras`` (``post_init_hook``): before this addon, the
Kras pipeline was the base addon's only behaviour. Uninstalling it sets
those connectors back to ``standard``, whose refresh fails closed. The
Kras settings (debt, untaxed and quotation fields) show on the connector
form only for ``kras`` connectors; collections managers can edit them
for their company.

Debt snapshot: the debt view (**Debt Scenario** and **Debt View**,
``Saldos al día/*``, one row per sales voucher) is a ~200 MB download. A
refresh downloads the customers and the debt view in one session, keeps
as open debt only vouchers with a positive local-currency balance
(``CVE_SALDOMONLOC > 0``; reconciled rows are dropped), and stores each
customer's open credit (the sum of its negative balances: credit notes,
advances) on the customer row. The sellers are the voucher sellers
(``CVEVEN_COD`` / ``VEN_DESC``) of every debt view row, open or not,
taken from that same download. Voucher type ``PRE`` (configurable
through Bejerman Quotation Kinds, default ``PRE``) is a ``quotation``,
every other type an ``invoice``; the external id is the stable
``type|point|letter|number`` key.

Untaxed amount (seller commission base): when **Untaxed Scenario** and
**Untaxed View** are set (a sales VAT book query view, e.g.
``Libro IVA Ventas/IA CRIS``; managers can edit both, setting only one
is a configuration error), the refresh reads that view in the same
session, after the debt view, for the last 365 days: the view's two
variable filters on the issue date are sent on every refresh, each by
the name the Queries parameters dialog shows: ``Fecha_Emision >=`` as
365 days ago and ``Fecha_Emision <=`` as today, ``DD/MM/YYYY``. Each
voucher is matched on its internal type (``COMP_CODINT``, not
``COMP_COD``), point of sale, letter and number (from ``COMPROBANTE``)
and customer (``CLIENTE_COD``); its untaxed share is
``(NETO + NGEX) / TOTAL``, so taxes and perceptions are left out. Every
open debt row stores ``amount_untaxed`` and ``balance_untaxed``, the
share of its amount and of its open balance (both shown, with totals, in
the **Bejerman Debt** list), and ``get_customer_debt`` sends
``amount_untaxed`` to ``collection``, which pays the seller's commission
on that share of what was collected. Only the open invoices that both
views list are kept: an invoice of ``Saldos al dia`` the VAT book view
does not list is left out of the debt, and the refresh logs how many and
a few of their ids. Quotations keep the total and are kept only when
issued in the same 365 days; older ones are left out and counted in the
log. When the view is not set, every open row is kept with its total. A
failed read of the view fails the refresh like the debt view.

Live debt: optionally, set Bejerman Debt Filter Field (for example
``CVE_CODCLI``) once the ERP exposes a filterable child view: debt is
then read live per customer instead of from the snapshot, with no
untaxed amount (commission on the total), and sheets marked **Entered in
the ERP** are no longer subtracted.
